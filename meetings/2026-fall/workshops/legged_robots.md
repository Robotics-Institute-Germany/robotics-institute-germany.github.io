# Legged Robots Reimagined: Towards General-Purpose Embodied Intelligence

- Time slot: Day 1, 09:00 - 12:00 h 
- Room: 2.046 (on the 2nd floor of Marchstrasse 23)

## Abstract

The workshop aims to bring together members of the RIG Legged Locomotion Cluster and its TCs, as well as interested researchers and industry partners from the wider German robotics community, to identify opportunities for joint research projects, collaborative proposals, student exchanges, and other forms of cooperation.

The focus is on the future of legged robots as capable, adaptive, and general-purpose embodied systems. Participants are invited to briefly pitch potential project ideas, highlighting the scientific challenge, their own contribution, and the complementary expertise, technologies, platforms, or partners required to pursue the idea.

Rather than a sequence of conventional presentations, the workshop will use short pitches followed by extended discussion and exchange to encourage matchmaking and the formation of new collaborations. The aim is to identify common research interests, connect complementary expertise across institutions and industry, and develop promising ideas into concrete joint project concepts, potential proposals, student exchanges, and longer-term collaborations.

The workshop will provide an open forum to discuss challenges and opportunities around legged locomotion, robot learning, control, robot dynamics and mechanics, autonomy, simulation-to-real transfer, robustness, and reliable operation, as well as application-driven challenges where legged robots can provide unique capabilities.

The ultimate goal is to move beyond individual project ideas and establish new collaborations within the RIG and with the broader robotics community in Germany, creating the basis for ambitious joint research proposals, mobility and exchange activities, and longer-term cooperation.

## Tentative Agenda


| Time | Session | Format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 09:00–09:05 | Welcome & Introduction | Workshop objectives, format and expected outcomes |
| 09:05–09:30 | Opening Pitches, Workshop Organizers | 3 × 5 min pitch + 3 min questions |

| 10:50–11:20 | Project / Call Pitches – Part II | 4× (6 min presentation + 2 min questions) |
| 11:20–12:00 | Discussion, Analysis & Planning | Identify synergies, form potential teams, discuss next steps and define follow-up actions |


Participants will be invited to present either:

- a new project idea or research concept;
- a proposal for collaboration around a specific funding call;
- an existing project idea looking for additional partners or complementary expertise; or
- an opportunity for student exchange, mobility, or other collaborative activities.

Each contribution will have 6 minutes for presentation + 2 minutes for questions.

The presenters for the first and second pitch sessions will be defined after the workshop invitation / registration, depending on the interest and proposed contributions.

The final session will focus on turning the ideas presented during the workshop into concrete opportunities for collaboration. Discussion will address:

- Which ideas have sufficient common interest to develop further?
- Which researchers, institutes, TCs, or companies could form complementary teams?
- Which ideas could be connected to specific funding calls?
- Where are additional partners or expertise needed?
- Are there opportunities for student exchange, researcher mobility, or shared activities?
- What are the immediate next steps, and who will coordinate them?
- The intended outcome is a short list of promising collaboration opportunities and concrete follow-up actions after the workshop.
